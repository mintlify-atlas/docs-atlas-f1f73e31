It detects bad form before it becomes an injury, speaks out loud when something's wrong, and logs your session history so you can track improvement over time.

## What GymGuard does

GymGuard is a GPU-accelerated fitness form coach that runs locally on your machine. Point your webcam at yourself, open your browser to `http://localhost:8000`, and GymGuard:

1. Detects and tracks 17 body keypoints at 20+ FPS using YOLOv8m-Pose
2. Automatically identifies which exercise you are performing (98% accuracy across 7 exercises)
3. Calculates joint angles and applies exercise-specific form rules
4. Scores your injury risk from 0 (safe) to 100 (stop immediately)
5. Speaks voice alerts through your browser when form breaks down

## Risk levels at a glance

| Score | Level | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | SAFE | Good form — keep going |
| 30–64 | WARNING | Deviations detected — reduce load |
| 65–100 | HIGH RISK | Stop and correct immediately |

<Note>
GymGuard requires Python 3.11+ and a webcam. An NVIDIA GPU with CUDA support is strongly recommended for smooth real-time performance. CPU-only mode works but runs at 5–8 FPS.
</Note>
